# Human Temperature Detection Using PiDog

**Status:** In progress. Collaborative project; individual contributions will be documented separately.

Objective: modify PiDog to acquire, process and communicate temperature measurements under defined experimental conditions. Hardware selection, implementation, performance and individual responsibilities remain to be confirmed. Educational prototype, not a clinically validated medical device.

Required deliverables: versioned code repository, 4 to 6 page report, 2 to 5 minute demonstration, 10 to 12 slides, ethical reflection and progress records. Final presentation: 10 to 12 minutes.
